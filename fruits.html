Fruits are the seed-bearing structures of flowering plants, offering essential nutrients for human health and vital mechanisms for plant reproduction. They are packed with vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants that boost immunity and fight disease. Their high fiber content supports gut health and digestion, while their natural water content aids hydration. Botanically, any seed-bearing part—including tomatoes, avocados, and cucumbers—is a fruit, making them an incredibly diverse and vital food group for daily energy and long-term wellness.
